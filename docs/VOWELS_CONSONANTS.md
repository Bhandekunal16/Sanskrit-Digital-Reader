# Sanskrit Vowels & Consonants (संस्कृत-वर्णमाला: स्वर एवं व्यञ्जन)

The **Vowels & Consonants (Varṇamālā)** module is a dedicated phonological and acoustic exploration suite located at `/vowels-consonants`. It allows learners to **watch** articulatory anatomy, understand character composition, and **hear** crystal-clear audio pronunciation for all 52 classical Sanskrit phonemes (*varṇas*).

---

## 🔤 1. Sanskrit Alphabet Taxonomy (५२ वर्ण)

Sanskrit phonology as codified in the *Śivasūtras* and *Pāṇinīya Śikṣā* organizes all sounds scientifically according to their physical point of articulation (*Sthāna*) and articulatory effort (*Prayatna*).

### A. Vowels (स्वर - Svaras: 14–16 Characters)
1. **Hrasva Svara (ह्रस्व स्वर / Short Vowels - 1 Mātrā)**:
   - `अ` (a), `इ` (i), `उ` (u), `ऋ` (ṛ), `ऌ` (ḷ)
2. **Dīrgha Svara (दीर्घ स्वर / Long Vowels - 2 Mātrās)**:
   - `आ` (ā), `ई` (ī), `ऊ` (ū), `ॠ` (ṝ), `ॡ` (ḹ)
3. **Sandhyakṣara (संयुक्त स्वर / Composite Diphthongs - 2 Mātrās)**:
   - `ए` (e) = a + i (Guṇa)
   - `ऐ` (ai) = ā + i (Vṛddhi)
   - `ओ` (o) = a + u (Guṇa)
   - `औ` (au) = ā + u (Vṛddhi)
4. **Ayogavāha (अयोगवाह / Dependent Modifiers)**:
   - `अं` (aṃ - Anusvāra / Pure nasal resonance)
   - `अः` (aḥ - Visarga / Voiceless glottal echo)

### B. Consonants (व्यञ्जन - Vyañjanas: 33 Standard Characters)
Organized into 5 *Vargas* (Sparśa stops) followed by Semivowels and Sibilants:

| Varga / Class | Place of Articulation | Unvoiced Unaspirated | Unvoiced Aspirated | Voiced Unaspirated | Voiced Aspirated | Nasal Stop |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **क-वर्ग (Ka-Varga)** | कण्ठ्य (Guttural/Velar) | **क** (ka) | **ख** (kha) | **ग** (ga) | **घ** (gha) | **ङ** (ṅa) |
| **च-वर्ग (Ca-Varga)** | तालव्य (Palatal) | **च** (ca) | **छ** (cha) | **ज** (ja) | **झ** (jha) | **ञ** (ña) |
| **ट-वर्ग (Ṭa-Varga)** | मूर्धन्य (Retroflex) | **ट** (ṭa) | **ठ** (ṭha) | **ड** (ḍa) | **ढ** (ḍha) | **ण** (ṇa) |
| **त-वर्ग (Ta-Varga)** | दन्त्य (Dental) | **त** (ta) | **थ** (tha) | **द** (da) | **ध** (dha) | **न** (na) |
| **प-वर्ग (Pa-Varga)** | ओष्ठ्य (Labial) | **प** (pa) | **फ** (pha) | **ब** (ba) | **भ** (bha) | **म** (ma) |

#### Antastha (अन्तस्थ / Semivowels / Approximants)
- `य` (ya - Palatal) · `र` (ra - Retroflex) · `ल` (la - Dental) · `व` (va - Dentolabial)

#### Ūṣman (ऊष्मन् / Sibilants & Aspirate)
- `श` (śa - Palatal Sibilant) · `ष` (ṣa - Retroflex Sibilant) · `स` (sa - Dental Sibilant) · `ह` (ha - Glottal Aspirate)

### C. Classical Conjuncts & Vedic Characters
- `क्ष` (kṣa) = क् + ष् + अ
- `त्र` (tra) = त् + र् + अ
- `ज्ञ` (jña) = ज् + ञ् + अ (Authentically pronounced *j-nya*)
- `श्र` (śra) = श् + र् + अ
- `ळ` (ḻa - Vedic Retroflex Lateral, e.g. Ṛgveda 1.1.1 *agnimīḻe*)

## 👁️ 2. Watch: Articulatory Vocal Tract & Character Studio

The visual studio provides three complementary perspectives:

1. **8 Points of Articulation (उच्चारण-स्थान दर्शन)**:
   - **कण्ठ (Kaṇṭha / Throat)**: `अ, आ, क, ख, ग, घ, ङ, ह, ः`
   - **तालु (Tālu / Hard Palate)**: `इ, ई, च, छ, ज, झ, ञ, य, श`
   - **मूर्धा (Mūrdhā / Palatal Dome)**: `ऋ, ॠ, ट, ठ, ड, ढ, ण, र, ष`
   - **दन्त (Danta / Upper Teeth)**: `ऌ, ॡ, त, थ, द, ध, न, ल, स`
   - **ओष्ठ (Oṣṭha / Lips)**: `उ, ऊ, प, फ, ब, भ, म`
   - **नासिका (Nāsikā / Nasal Cavity)**: `ङ, ञ, ण, न, म, ं, ँ`
   - **कण्ठतालु (Kaṇṭha-Tālu / Dual)**: `ए, ऐ`
   - **कण्ठोष्ठ (Kaṇṭhoṣṭha / Dual)**: `ओ, औ`
   - **दन्तोष्ठ (Dantoṣṭha / Dual)**: `व`

2. **Akṣara Syllable Construction**:
   - Demonstrates the algebraic formation of Sanskrit syllables:
     $$\text{क् (Pure Consonant Base)} + \text{ा (Vocalic Mātrā)} = \text{का (Syllable)}$$

3. **Varṇa Inspector**:
   - Renders high-contrast Devanagari typography, IAST, IPA, internal effort (*Ābhyantara Prayatna*), external effort (*Bāhya Prayatna*), Pāṇinian sūtra rule, and exemplar vocabulary with real-world translations.

## 🔊 3. Hear: Dual-Engine Audio Pronunciation Architecture

Located in `src/lib/phoneticsAudio.ts`, the audio system operates using two synchronized acoustic layers:

1. **Web Audio API Acoustic Formant Synthesizer**:
   - Generates human vocal tract resonance using parallel bandpass formant filters ($F_1$, $F_2$, $F_3$).
   - Calculates vowel height ($F_1$), tongue advancement ($F_2$), lip rounding ($F_3$), aspiration white-noise bursts, and glottal fundamental frequency ($F_0 \approx 135-145\text{ Hz}$).
   - Guarantees **zero latency** and works completely offline without network requests.

2. **Web Speech API Indic TTS**:
   - Selects native Sanskrit/Hindi/Marathi speech synthesis voices (`hi-IN`, `sa-IN`, `mr-IN`) with calibrated pitch and playback rate.

3. **Guided Autoplay Tour (स्वर-गान / व्यञ्जन-गान)**:
   - Plays all vowels or consonants sequentially with visual auto-advancing spotlights and selectable study speeds:
     - `0.5x` (Slow Study Mode)
     - `0.75x` (Careful Articulation)
     - `1.0x` (Natural Vedic Cadence)

## 🎧 6. Ear Training & Listening Practice (श्रवण-अभ्यास)

An interactive acoustic discrimination trainer that plays randomized Sanskrit sounds and tests the user's ability to distinguish subtle phonetic contrasts:
- **Retroflex vs Dental** (ट vs त, ड vs द, ठ vs थ, ण vs न)
- **Aspirated vs Unaspirated** (क vs ख, ग vs घ, च vs छ, प vs फ)
- **Sibilant Triad** (तालव्य श vs मूर्धन्य ष vs दन्त्य स)
- **Vocalic Vowels** (ऋ, ॠ, ऌ)

Includes streak tracking, scoring, and detailed phonetic explanations upon answer submission.
